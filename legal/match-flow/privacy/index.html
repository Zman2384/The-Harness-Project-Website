MATCH Flow Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Last updated October 22, 2026 · Published by The HARNESS Project LLC

MATCH Flow is built local-first. There is no account or login. Plans, Ghost Audits and chat history are saved on your device. Optional live Lil Z replies send the context described below for AI processing.

The Zero Stigma Tax model

You can use the planning tools and Lil Z’s built-in briefings without an account or sending chat content. Live AI is a separate, optional choice that starts off on each visit. It does not sync your local records, but it does process the chat context you agree to send.

  • No account creation, email address, phone number, or password.
  • No advertising identifiers, ad SDKs, or data brokers.
  • No sale or rental of personal information. Optional live AI shares context for response processing.
  • No behavioral profiling, cross-app tracking, or lookalike audiences.
  • No requirement that you identify yourself to use any feature.

What stays on your device

The following records are saved in local app storage. Optional live AI can receive a summary of plan selections and activity counts, not the private notes in these records:

  • Your consent-gate confirmations and MATCH plan selections.
  • SafER Score answers, results, and history.
  • Session records, check-ins, anxiety-log entries, and ritual streaks.
  • Saved and custom scripts, and your decision-flow builder nodes.
  • Emergency and safety-network contact names, numbers, and relationships.
  • Saved clinics, reminder preferences, and any free-text note you write.

Optional live Lil Z replies

Lil Z starts with on-device briefings. If you turn on Live AI replies, the app sends up to 14 recent non-crisis chat turns, a structured MATCH plan summary, activity counts, and saved memories (when memory is enabled) through our AI service at theharnessproject.org to Anthropic, which runs the Claude model that writes Lil Z’s replies. Our service passes the request through; its code does not store or log your messages. Cloudflare, which hosts the service, processes each request to deliver it under its own privacy policy. Your chat can contain sensitive information if you type it. Do not include names, partner identifiers or other details you do not want processed outside your device.

  • Live AI permission is not saved between visits. Turning it off stops future requests, not processing of an earlier request.
  • Detected immediate-danger and self-harm messages receive an on-device crisis card and are excluded from live requests. Detection cannot identify every possible crisis phrase.
  • Clear conversation deletes local chat history. Turn memory off or remove individual memories to delete saved facts. These controls cannot delete information already sent to an AI provider.
  • Anthropic’s commercial terms and privacy policy (https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) apply to live processing. We do not promise zero retention or that every provider copy can be erased through this app.

What we never collect

No name, email, address, account or identifying profile is required. We do not read your contacts list or photos. Names and phone numbers entered in your safety network stay in local records. Information you type in Lil Z may be sent for processing only when you enable live replies.

Device permissions

Where the App asks for a permission, it is used for the feature you choose. Clinic Finder uses location only as described below; local reminders do not.

  • Phone dialler — to place a call you tap in the emergency sheet. We do not read your call log.
  • Notifications — to show local reminders scheduled on your device. Reminder content never leaves the device.
  • Location — accessed only when you tap Use current location in Clinic Finder. Your position is turned into the nearest ZIP code on your device; the coordinates are never sent anywhere or saved. The App does not save your ZIP, address or search history.

Third parties

Outside services receive only the requests needed for features you choose. Optional live AI is the exception to on-device chat processing described above.

  • Platform stores (Apple, Google) handle distribution, crash reporting you opt into at the OS level, and any purchase. Their own privacy policies apply to those interactions.
  • Clinic Finder links to official locators and to Google Maps. Only when you tap Open map search does the App send your ZIP or typed address and the type of care to Google Maps; your plans, Ghost Audits and notes are never sent. Google’s Terms of Service (https://policies.google.com/terms) and Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) apply. When you tap Open finder after entering a ZIP, that ZIP may be included in the official finder’s link (for example, HRSA Find a Health Center). Official finders open in your browser under their own policies.
  • Fonts are loaded from a public font CDN on the web build, which sees a standard web request.
  • Product analytics are not enabled in released builds. Anonymous, non-identifying usage counters may run only inside our internal development preview and are never linked to a person or to any content you enter.

Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Local planning does not send your records to our systems. Optional live AI processing starts only after you enable it and can be stopped for future messages. Clinic Finder sends nothing until you open a map search or finder link. Link and font requests also involve technical data. Privacy questions and requests about live processing can be sent to our privacy contact; provider handling is subject to the applicable provider policy.

Your rights under GDPR

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and the right not to be subject to solely automated decision-making. In this App those rights are exercised directly and instantly:

  • Access and rectification — every stored value is visible and editable in the screen that created it.
  • Erasure — Settings → "Wipe all local data" clears the app’s local records. Uninstalling removes native app storage, but separate backups and previously sent live AI requests are outside that control. On the web, clear the site’s storage; removing a shortcut alone does not clear it.
  • Restriction and objection — stop using a feature, or revoke its permission in your OS settings.
  • Portability — data is held in a standard local store on your device; we do not withhold it behind an export gate.
  • Complaint — you may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority at any time.

California rights (CCPA / CPRA)

For California residents:

  • We do not sell personal information and have not in the preceding 12 months.
  • We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Live AI may process sensitive information you choose to send. Turn it off to keep future chat messages on-device, and contact us about processing-related privacy requests.
  • Your rights to know, correct, delete, and limit use of sensitive information are satisfied by the on-device controls described above, and you may also contact us to make a formal request.
  • We will never discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

Children (COPPA)

The App is rated for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13, and the App is not intended for anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the App on a device you control, wipe the local data in Settings and remove the App.

Security

Your data is protected by your device: its lock screen, its encryption, and the operating system’s app sandbox. Keep your device passcode enabled and its software up to date. Note that an unencrypted device backup made by your computer or cloud provider may include App data; that backup is governed by your backup provider, not by us.

Retention and international transfers

Local records remain until you clear them or remove native app storage. Live AI requests may be processed outside your country, and the AI provider’s retention policy applies. Turning off live AI or clearing local data does not recall earlier requests.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will update the "last updated" date and surface material changes in the App. Privacy questions and formal requests: email@theharnessproject.org.
