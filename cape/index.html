What CAPE stands for

Read it as text

The Core Mantra: Your CAPE is yours to wear — every single day.

C

Confident

Knowing your worth and trusting your instincts before any pressure arrives.

A

Aware

Reading your surroundings and tuning into your own internal radar in real time.

P

Protected

Having the tools, team, and plans built out that keep you safe.

E

Empowered

Leading your life with healthy habits every single day, not just in a crisis.

The MATCH Compass: a Decision GPS

Five points students can check in any situation — at a party, in a group chat, or at the clinic front desk.

See MATCH in both languages →

  • Health ArmorM · My Medical / Health Choices

    Annual check-ups, vaccines like HPV and Hep B, and knowing your health baseline.

  • Built-In RadarA · Awareness

    Trusting your gut, reading the room, and catching warning signs early.

  • Safety KitT · Tools & Trusted Support

    A charged phone, a ride plan, a safety buddy, and 3–5 trusted adults on speed dial.

  • Strongest VoiceC · Clear Communication & Consent

    Rehearsed boundary scripts and the FRIES consent model.

  • Daily SuperpowerH · Healthy Habits / Choosing Your Pace

    Sleep, movement, choosing your own pace, and protecting your permanent digital legacy.

Two tracks, matched to age

Content is split by grade band so every lesson fits where students are developmentally.

Grades 6–8

Green Track

  • Confidence, boundaries, and consent
  • The 3-Second Rule before sending anything online
  • Consent Café role-plays with boundary menus
  • The Digital Handoff: saving your plan before the class ends
Grades 9–12

Blue Track

  • Everything in Green Track, deepened
  • Protection and medical shields
  • Navigating clinics, insurance, and confidentiality
  • Naming the Ghosts and the Clinical Bridge: words for the front desk
  • The Green Light Pivot (Part 5)

Blue Track content is gated. Protection and clinical modules are never delivered to Green Track students, and are taught only with institutional approval.

The CAPE Promise

For parents and guardians

You should know exactly what your child is learning. CAPE is built so families can see the materials before a lesson is taught.

  • Preview first. The CAPE book includes a Parent & Educator Guide, and a parent preview sample and family communication templates are available on request.
  • Age-gated content. Protection topics are Blue Track only (grades 9–12).
  • Waiting is a valid choice. Choosing your own pace is taught as a leadership decision.
  • Safe rooms. Every session has two adults present, and facilitators are background-checked mandated reporters.
