Science as Your Wingman

MATCH is five interlocking layers that keep working even when one fails.

  • AAwareness Turn fear into data with the Ghost Hunting Anxiety Audit
  • TTools Frontline gear: condoms, lube, and barriers that actually get used
  • CCommunication Saying what you need without killing the vibe
  • HHabits Small, repeatable acts that make you fearless

The Digital Decision GPS, up close

Core concept

Your Digital Decision GPS

  • Anxiety to agencyNot a static rulebook: a live navigation system for real-world social and digital gray areas, built on confidence and self-respect.
  • Building muscle memoryClassroom health concepts become real-time power moves for the moments that matter: group chats, sleepovers, and plans that fall apart.
  • Protection begins with youSelf-worth comes first, before any tool or partner is part of the picture.

The MATCH Compass

5 Decision GPS superpowers

  • M
    Health ArmorMy Medical / Health ChoicesAnnual check-ups, vaccines (HPV, Hep B), and knowing your health baseline.
  • A
    Built-In RadarAwarenessReading the room, trusting your gut as real data, and spotting sketchy vibes early.
  • T
    Safety KitTools & Trusted SupportPrep before high-pressure moments: a charged phone, a ride plan, a safety buddy, and trusted adults on speed dial.
  • C
    Strongest VoiceClear Communication & ConsentBoundary scripts and the FRIES consent model: Freely given, Reversible, Informed, Enthusiastic, Specific.
  • H
    Daily SuperpowerHealthy Habits / Choosing Your PaceDaily self-care, choosing your own pace (abstinence is a leadership move), and protecting your digital legacy.

Core app feature suite

Four tools in your pocket

  • Digital Ghost VaultA private space to name specific fears, like group-chat pressure, rejection or phone anxiety, and map each one to a shield strategy.
  • Strongest Voice Script LabWord-for-word, FRIES-based boundary scripts for high-friction moments like “The Midnight Text.”
  • Stress Test SimulatorPractice the Pivot: switch to a backup when the first plan fails, like a ride that falls through or a dead phone battery.
  • Tiered Track ToggleAge-gated content. Green Track (grades 6–8): mindset, boundaries and digital safety. Blue Track (grades 9–12) unlocks medical shields and frontline gear.
