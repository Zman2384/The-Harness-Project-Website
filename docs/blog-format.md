# The Hook-Pivot Model: 1 a.m. Reality Blog Format
Nov 10, 2026 · Christopher Zacharie
Source of truth for the weekly blog agent. Repo copy of https://claude.ai/artifact/CyyfCLCy3uiBTDanuPAKhC — keep the two in sync. Every post follows this order.

## 1. Post header
- **Lane masthead/banner:** the lane's banner, cropped to the framed core (title, flame, tagline). For CAPE and youth posts, drop "The HARNESS Blog".
- **Hero image:** 16:9, in the lane's skin.
- **Lane tag:** Adults, Youth & Family, Parents & Caregivers, or Educators.
- **Title**
- **Byline:** Christopher Zacharie · date · reading time in minutes · The 1 a.m. Reality

## 2. Beat 1: The Hook (the 1 a.m. moment)
- **Label and time stamp:** "The 1 a.m. Reality" on adult posts, "The moment" on the other lanes, plus a time stamp (e.g. SAT · 1:14 A.M.).
- **The scene:** a real, specific moment, 2–4 short paragraphs, told in second person. Something just broke or someone is stuck, the clinic is closed, and the brain jumps to the worst case.
- **End on the tension.** Don't fix anything yet.

## 3. The Pivot line
One bridge sentence that names the framework:
- **Adults →** MATCH Framework (the modular puzzle: which piece broke, which pieces still hold). No GPS language.
- **Youth & Family / Parents & Caregivers →** CAPE Decision GPS (compass and route language). No puzzle or harness imagery.
- **Educators →** the teaching move: how to run this moment in the room with fidelity.

## 4. Beat 2: The body
- **Short paragraphs:** 2–3 sentences each, with H2s every few paragraphs so it's easy to scan.
- **Name the ghost:** the fear driving the bad choice (Timing, Cost, Disclosure, Rejection…).
- **The fix:** the facts, the clock and the options, with one source link for any clinical claim.
- **The script:** one line the reader can say under pressure, in a styled callout or quote.
- **Local waypoints** when relevant (Houston resources).

## 5. Key takeaway
One sentence in a highlighted box, the line you'd want them to remember at 1 a.m.

## 6. "Bring it home" (closing)
- **Summary:** a 2–3 sentence recap.
- **Question box:** "Today's question" on adult posts, "Reflect" on the other lanes. One open question.

## 7. Disclaimer footer (mandatory, word for word)
> The HARNESS Project LLC | theharnessproject.org. For educational purposes only. Not medical advice. © 2026 Christopher Zacharie and The HARNESS Project LLC. All rights reserved.

## 8. After the article
- **Author card**
- **Lane call to action:**
  - Adults → Get The Signal (Kit link)
  - Youth → Bring CAPE to your school (grades 6–12, TEKS)
  - Educators → Start certification
  - Parents & Caregivers → follows the same pattern, with a lane-appropriate call to action.
- **Brave Space comments: adult posts only.** Infographic → Today's Question → 6 ground-rule checklist that must be ticked before the comment form unlocks. Never on youth posts.
- **Navigation:** All posts and Next post buttons.

## Lane skins
- **Adults:** Digital Health Armor starfield, with the flame medallion crest.
- **Youth & Family:** Tactical Notebook cream graph paper, with the cape-over-compass crest.
- **Parents & Caregivers:** cream notebook, with the house crest.
- **Educators:** Clinical Blueprint slate grid, with the Certified Facilitator seal.

## Blog specs
- **Track:** both. CAPE posts and HARNESS posts, labeled per post.
- **Where it lives:** the website, public and indexed for SEO.
- **Job:** get found, earn trust, bring readers in.
- **Scope:** one scenario, one tool, done deep.
- **Length:** 800–1,200 words.
- **Structure:** hero image, short paragraphs, styled script callouts, summary plus closing question, attribution and disclaimer footer.
- **Skin:** site skin. Tactical Notebook for CAPE posts.
- **Interaction:** Brave Space comment section on HARNESS posts only.
- **Cadence:** evergreen, posted as written.
- **SEO:** full metadata, keyword clusters, schema.

## How it differs from The Signal
The blog is one story moving from the hook to the pivot to the fix, and it has four lanes. The Signal is adults-only and uses the fixed sections: Drill → Radar [A] → Voice [C] → Agency [M].
